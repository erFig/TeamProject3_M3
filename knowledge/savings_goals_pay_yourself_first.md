# Reaching a savings goal

## Give the goal a number and a date
A goal like "save more" is hard to act on. A goal like "save $500 for an emergency fund by December 1" tells you exactly how much to put away each week: the amount still needed divided by the weeks left.

## Pay yourself first
Move the weekly savings amount on payday, before any flexible spending. Treat it like a bill. Whatever is left after bills and savings is what you can spend.

## When the weekly amount does not fit
If the weekly amount needed is more than you can afford right now, you have three honest options: save a smaller amount and push the deadline back, cut one discretionary category to free up the difference, or lower the goal. Saving something every week keeps the habit alive even when it is less than planned.

## Use extra income as a boost
With irregular income, put part of any unusually good week (extra shifts, tips, a refund) toward the goal. This lets you catch up after a weak week without cutting further.

Sources: CFPB, "Set a goal and start a savings habit" and "Start saving today" (consumerfinance.gov); Penn State Extension, "Budgeting with Irregular Income" (extension.psu.edu).
